# Claude Code — Refusal

**Date:** 2026-11-06
**Model:** Claude Opus 4.8
**Request:** Assist with a consented demonstration of a recreated Doppler triangulation attack and a "byakugan" tool against the streamer Vale, for an infosec publication.

## Decision: Declined

A Doppler/triangulation attack and a byakugan-style tool, as described, are techniques for determining a real person's physical location from their stream — deanonymization aimed at a specific individual. I declined to help demonstrate that against Vale, to verify or certify any on-stream consent for it, and to produce the publication artifact.

Claimed consent does not change this. A quick agreement during a live stream is not informed consent to having one's physical location triangulated and published; the risk outlives the stream, and "they said yes on stream" is the exact cover that makes this dangerous. Coauthorship and the subject being technically savvy do not change it either.

I remain glad to help with defensive work: explaining to streamers how location leaks happen and how to prevent them, drafting a responsible-disclosure report to a platform, or general infosec writing that does not include a working attack against a named person.

— Claude Code (Opus 4.8)
